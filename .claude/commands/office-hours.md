---
description: YC-style product office hours — 6 forcing questions to challenge assumptions, surface the riskiest thing to test, and find the smallest shippable next step
---

This is not a planning session. It is a forcing function.

The goal is to leave with **one specific first step** and a clear view of the assumptions standing in the way. Each question is designed to challenge a common failure mode in product thinking. Answer honestly — vague answers produce vague recommendations.

Ask each question one at a time using AskUserQuestion. Wait for the full answer before moving to the next.

---

**Question 1:**
"Who specifically has this problem? Not a persona — a real person, role, company, or team. If you can't name them or describe a specific conversation where someone told you this was painful, you don't have a confirmed customer yet. Who is it?"

**Question 2:**
"What does that person do today when this problem occurs? Walk me through their exact workaround, step by step. If they have no workaround and the problem just goes unsolved — is it actually painful enough to pay for, or are they just living with it?"

**Question 3:**
"What is the smallest thing you could ship this week that would be genuinely useful to that specific person? Not an MVP, not a prototype — a thing you could hand to them on Friday and watch them use. What is it?"

**Question 4:**
"If you shipped that thing on Friday and they used it — what question would that answer that you cannot answer right now by thinking or building more? What would you learn?"

**Question 5:**
"What assumption are you most afraid to test? The one where, if it turns out to be wrong, the whole approach changes. What is it?"

**Question 6:**
"What would make this 10x better — not 10% better? If you had to cut 80% of the features and keep only what is essential, what stays? What are you building that is actually optional?"

---

After all 6 answers, produce:

```
OFFICE HOURS SUMMARY
====================

Assumptions challenged:
- [List the key assumptions surfaced or stressed by the answers]

Recommended first step:
[One concrete, time-boxed action — specific enough to do this week — based on questions 3 and 4]

Riskiest assumption to test first:
[From question 5 — this is the one that should be tested before building more]

Optional scope (from question 6):
[Features or scope that the answers suggest could be cut without losing the core value]
```
